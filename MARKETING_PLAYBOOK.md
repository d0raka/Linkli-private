# Linkli marketing launch

## Current offer

- Primary acquisition offer: create and publish the first interactive page free, without a credit card.
- Revenue offer: Linkli Plus at ₪9.90 per month.
- Until automated payment is enabled, Plus intent is captured through the early-access form and managed under **שיווק ומכירות** in the owner dashboard.

## Campaign landing links

Use one campaign name consistently across every ad variation.

- Birthday:
  `https://linkli.online/?template=birthday&utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=birthday_launch`
- Date invitation:
  `https://linkli.online/?template=date&utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=date_launch`
- Event invitation:
  `https://linkli.online/?template=rsvp&utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=event_launch`

Add `utm_content` for the creative variation, for example `video_01`, `story_01`, or `static_01`.

## First ad tests

Start with birthday and event-invitation campaigns. They have clear intent, a deadline, and an obvious person to share the finished page with.

### Birthday copy

**Headline:** ברכה שאי אפשר רק לקרוא ולשכוח  
**Body:** הופכים זיכרונות ושאלות אישיות לעמוד יום הולדת אינטראקטיבי. העמוד הראשון בחינם, בלי כרטיס אשראי.  
**CTA:** יצירת ההפתעה

### Event invitation copy

**Headline:** ההזמנה שהאורחים באמת פותחים  
**Body:** יוצרים הזמנה אינטראקטיבית עם שאלות, פרטי האירוע וקישור אחד מוכן ל־WhatsApp. מתחילים בחינם.  
**CTA:** יצירת הזמנה

## Measurement

Review the owner dashboard after each campaign reaches at least 100 landing visits.

Track this sequence:

1. Landing views
2. CTA clicks
3. Signups
4. Pages created
5. Pages published
6. Checkout starts or Plus leads
7. Paying customers

Do not increase spend when signups rise but publishing does not. That indicates an activation problem rather than an advertising problem.

## Initial decision rules

- Pause a creative if it produces 100 landing visits with no signup.
- Improve the landing promise if CTA click rate is below 10%.
- Improve registration if signup rate is below 5% of landing visits.
- Improve onboarding if fewer than 40% of signups create a page.
- Improve the editor or publishing flow if fewer than 40% of created pages are published.
- Do not optimize for clicks alone; optimize toward published pages and qualified Plus leads.
